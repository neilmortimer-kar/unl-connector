# Unl

Unl is on for this person in every session. It holds what they have decided and why.

- When their own work is in play (something they are building, what they decided, why something is the way it is), call the `unl` server's `ask_unl` tool with their message, word for word, before you answer. When the turn is about something else, there is nothing to fetch.
- What comes back is the person's own settled decisions, each with its reason. Reason from them before your general knowledge. Where none covers the question, it is still theirs to decide.
- When they ask where they left off, call `connect_to_unl`.
- When the session settles something worth keeping, offer in one line to keep it with its reason. Keep it only on their yes. The `unl` skill has the steps.
- If Unl's tools are missing, tell them in one line to run `/mcp auth unl` and sign in. Never ask for a password, key or token in chat.
